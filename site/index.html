The first ninety days.

A fixed start so you can judge us quickly. After week thirteen it rolls monthly, with thirty days’ notice either way.

Week01of 13

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Audit

    We go through your ad accounts, analytics and margins line by line, and tell you what we would stop doing first.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Plan

    A ninety-day plan with budgets by channel, the tests we will run, and the number we agree to be judged on.

  3. Weeks 5–8

    Make and test

    The studio shoots and edits the first batch. Media launches it in small, cheap tests and reports back every week.

  4. Weeks 9–13

    Scale

    Winners get the budget, losers get cut, and the next batch of creative is briefed from what the data showed.
